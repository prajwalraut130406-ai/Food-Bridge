FoodBridge

Good food should reach a plate.

FoodBridge connects hotels with surplus meals to NGOs and volunteers who get them to people who need them.

  1. Hotels post surplus foodItems, quantity, photo and pickup time.
  2. NGOs claim what they can serveThe first claim reserves the donation.
  3. Volunteers collect and deliverOTP checks at pickup and drop-off keep it safe.
Every delivery earns the volunteer points and a certificate.
